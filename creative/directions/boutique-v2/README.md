# Bariga — contemporary specialist boutique, v0.2

Status: revised art-direction proposal, not an approved design or production implementation.

Client response: this was considered a possibility, with a request to make it more boutique. The selected refinement character is intimate, editorial and quietly distinctive. See [v0.3](../boutique-v3/README.md); this version remains preserved for comparison.

## Client feedback and interpretation

The client rejected all three initial directions because they did not feel like a good firm. When asked to distinguish formal corporate presence from a contemporary specialist boutique, the client chose **contemporary specialist boutique**, then authorized continued exploration.

The earlier inference that the client wanted a larger institutional/corporate presence is superseded. The intended character is a distinctive, accomplished boutique with deliberate visual craft. It should feel like a cohesive law firm, while retaining its actual scale and factual basis.

The first round's weaknesses were an overly brochure-like portrait composition, a diagram that resembled a consulting presentation, and a directory-like service index. The initial A recommendation is withdrawn. Those boards are archived as rejected exploration.

The masthead gives the existing BLF identity and firm name meaningful visual presence. The opening combines the original values headline and introduction with a full-width architectural image. The light practice section establishes reading hierarchy beyond the hero. The partner portrait is proposed for a substantive About treatment rather than the homepage's primary image.

The palette follows the original blue mark, off-white reading surfaces, charcoal/slate imagery and a limited warm reflection within the photograph. The type direction is a substantial contemporary serif paired with clean reading text. Exact font selection is still open; the generated images do not certify font-family identity.

## Content and asset invariants

The prompts use the existing headline, full firm introduction, action labels, featured-practice heading and two existing practice descriptions. The proposal changes composition, not the firm's offer. No rankings, awards, years, client logos, case outcomes, team members or new qualifications are added.

The source logo was supplied as a reference for the generated compositions. These raster interpretations do not establish pixel-perfect logo reproduction. Production must use the untouched `creative/old_website/logo_bargia.png` artwork. Exact text must likewise come from the reviewed content source rather than being transcribed from the generated images; punctuation and raster text remain subject to final copy comparison.

The photograph is conceptual and AI-generated. Its role is atmosphere, not evidence of an office, location, owned property or actual firm facilities. Any future use must preserve that distinction. Its final selection and crop are still proposed.

## Proposed continuation across pages

- Home: the firm identity and existing offer, then selective practice content and routes onward.
- About: a considered portrait/profile composition, with the existing managing-partner message given enough space to read. Actual credentials remain source-bound.
- Practice Areas: the same disciplined hierarchy with all seven original descriptions and useful direct section links; choose a layout after this art direction is accepted.
- Layanan Strategis: explain the three-domain relationship through a dedicated composition, using its existing wording rather than the rejected diagram-led homepage.
- Consultation: an accessible destination for the existing process and agreed contact channels. Form delivery and the final page label remain unresolved.

No full-page design has been approved. The five-page structure remains proposed, and this refinement does not authorize new content or functionality.

## Motion proposal, not observed behavior

Begin with the firm identity and key information available. A small shift of light/crop within the architectural image could introduce atmosphere as the opening settles. The hero-to-practice handoff would bring the reader from a dark image into a light, stable reading surface while retaining the original section heading. This must not delay contact actions or require a completed introduction.

Other pages should share the brand rhythm without replaying the homepage image on every route. Direct entry and Back/Forward must remain ordinary usable journeys. A reduced-motion treatment presents stable complete content. Phone crops and reading order need an independent authored treatment, not a scaled desktop screenshot. Detailed timing, interruption and device behavior await a later bounded study.

## Evidence and limitations

Both mockups and the mood image were generated with the built-in imagegen tool and visually inspected in this conversation. They provide rendered raster composition evidence; they are not browser screenshots, working interfaces, exact viewport simulations, motion recordings or proof of responsive behavior. No application source, dependencies or production site was changed.

An attempted isolated-browser capture of reference sites was not executed because automatic approval review could not complete after reaching its usage limit. The failure was not a finding that browsing was unsafe. No retry bypass was attempted. Reference-site rendering and local browser validation of this proposal remain pending.

The official pages of [Pallas](https://pallasllp.com/), [Selendy Gay](https://www.selendygay.com/) and [Three Crowns](https://www.threecrownsllp.com/) were read for how they describe the firm and organize substantive material. This was text/source inspection, not a visual or motion benchmark. Their branding, achievements, imagery and copy were not transferred to Bariga.
