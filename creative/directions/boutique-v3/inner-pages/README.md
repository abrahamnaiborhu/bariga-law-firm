## Evidence and limits

Original logo and partner photograph must be used directly in production. Image-generated representations can change their details and are unsuitable as replacements. Exact text comes from the baseline, not transcription of these images. Original source credentials and claims have not been independently verified.

These images demonstrate composition only. They do not establish responsive behavior, interaction, motion, keyboard access, browser support or performance. Exact typefaces, compact-laptop treatment and connected navigation still need production planning and verification. Preferred visual direction does not imply approval of every detail.

Files ending `-draft.png` are superseded studies; use the eight links above for review. Prompt text files alongside the images record the generation briefs and copy corrections. Homepage architectural imagery is conceptual decoration and is not represented as the firm's premises.
