# Bariga — intimate editorial boutique, v0.3

Status: focused refinement for client review, not an approved design or production sprint.

## Confirmed steering

The client regarded v0.2 as a possibility and asked for a more boutique character. They then selected **intimate, editorial and quietly distinctive**, rather than a bolder expressive direction. Keep the existing logo, most current wording, complete visual redesign and multipage requirement.

## View the refinement

- [Desktop concept](desktop-concept.png).
- [Phone concept](phone-concept.png).
- [Previous desktop](../boutique-v2/desktop-concept.png) and [previous phone](../boutique-v2/phone-concept.png), retained for comparison.

The new composition uses a calmer brand lockup, warm light reading surfaces, an italic emphasis on the existing word “Strategy,” a more contained architectural image and a stacked practice-content rhythm. The desktop gives the typography and introduction their own light surface beside the image. The phone places the complete introduction and both actions before a horizontal photographic crop, then continues into practice content.

The firm remains the subject; no partner portrait is introduced into the opening. The original firm introduction, headline, action labels, practice-section heading and two featured-practice descriptions remain the copy baseline. No new claims, biography, services or achievements were added.

## Recommendation and remaining refinement

Recommend developing this quieter editorial direction, subject to the client's reaction. Carry the considered image framing, reading rhythm and brand proportions into substantive inner pages. The About page still needs its own portrait/message composition; strategic services and consultation also need specific treatments.

The generated desktop retains an outlined primary action while the phone uses the quieter underlined link treatment. Recommend testing a consistent, clearly actionable link treatment in the next composition study. Exact typography, spacing, compact-laptop fit and long-form reading remain to be resolved with deterministic layouts. These are refinement items, not evidence of a working responsive implementation.

Motion is still a proposal: a subtle change of crop/light can add atmosphere to imagery while reading surfaces and actions remain stable. Page transitions should preserve orientation and allow immediate navigation. The source content must remain complete with reduced motion; no timed introduction or scroll lock is approved.

## Provenance and limits

Both compositions were generated with the built-in imagegen tool and visually inspected. They are raster art-direction studies, not browser screenshots, verified viewport simulations or working pages. No application source or dependencies changed, and no browser validation was attempted in this refinement.

The existing logo was supplied as a reference. Its final implementation must use the untouched original artwork; generated raster interpretation is not proof of pixel identity. Production copy must come from the reviewed source, not OCR or transcription of these images. Exact font-family metadata is unverified.

The architectural image is reused from v0.2 as conceptual decorative imagery, not a depiction of Bariga's premises. The original source and [image prompt](../boutique-v2/assets/architectural-light-prompt.txt) remain preserved there. Native imagegen was used throughout; no CLI/API fallback was used.

Final prompts for this refinement: [desktop](desktop-prompt.txt) and [phone](phone-prompt.txt). The generated originals are retained at their default location and project copies are saved here. No direction or implementation sprint has been approved by this refinement request alone.
