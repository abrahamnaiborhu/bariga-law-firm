# Bariga Law Firm — website plan v0.3

Status: proposed page structure and planning sequence. This is not an approved production sprint.

## Confirmed direction

- Preserve the substance and most of the existing wording.
- Build a website with separate pages.
- Retain the existing logo.
- Deliver a complete visual redesign: fresh layouts, typography, colors, image treatments, navigation presentation and motion language.
- Use the agreed React + Vite stack.

The existing website already has four main pages. Give each a clearer responsibility and propose a dedicated consultation destination using available content. The user explicitly rejects the current visual design. Treat the old site as factual/content evidence; its composition and styling have no approved carryover status. The existing logo is the confirmed visual asset to retain. Photographs remain available for new art direction; their current framing and placement are not requirements.

## Visual redesign requirement

Develop a new visual identity around the retained logo and existing subject matter. Review materially different concepts through attention, composition, image use, navigation and motion, rather than offering palette swaps of the current website. Existing teal circles, gradients, ornate type, stock legal imagery, equal card grids and entrance effects are not design starting points. Any reuse must earn its place through the selected new concept.

Use the real copy to judge each concept. Show an opening and meaningful inner-page content at desktop, phone and compact-laptop sizes. Text preservation must influence readable composition, not force reproduction of the existing arrangement. Detailed motion follows inspectable composition and a selected experiential direction. This requirement authorizes creative planning; production remains subject to the existing workflow.

## Content placement, with minimal rewriting

The lists below identify content ownership and proposed journeys. They do not fix visual section order, columns, card patterns, hero layouts or presentation. Composition may be reorganized substantially while preserving the wording and meaning.

### Home

1. Keep the existing opening headline and introduction as the first composition study's copy baseline. The client has not approved replacing them with a new positioning statement.
2. Retain the four featured practice names/descriptions. Make their links reach the relevant practice sections.
3. Retain the strategic-partnership heading/paragraph and a partner portrait. Link the existing approach action to About Us.
4. Retain the consultation invitation as the closing next step. Propose moving the detailed process to the dedicated consultation page; retain immediate direct-contact access.

This keeps the homepage useful as an introduction and index without repeating every detail page. Any excerpt or shortened paragraph must be shown for copy review rather than silently rewritten.

### About Us

1. Retain the firm introduction and its three pillars.
2. Make the portrait and full managing partner message a central composition.
3. Retain the explanation of the legal, financial and management relationship.
4. Provide clear onward routes to the strategic-services page and consultation.

A longer professional biography is optional future content, not a prerequisite to preserve the current message. Do not create team profiles, experience metrics or credentials beyond approved source material.

### Practice Areas

Retain the introduction and all seven descriptions: civil/business; insolvency and PKPU; general/business criminal; arbitration/dispute resolution; family/inheritance; banking/finance; technology/data protection. Preserve individual-client services alongside the business offerings. Give each a readable section, a shareable destination and a useful route to consultation.

The existing invitation to explore business strategy can become a concise cross-link to Layanan Strategis. Review any removal of repeated paragraphs in the content map before implementation.

### Layanan Strategis

Retain the current training/consulting introduction, diagram labels and three descriptions: Business Process Training, Finance & Restructuring, Law & Compliance Training. The existing opening already explains that these complement the firm's legal services; preserve that distinction.

Redesign the diagram's presentation so its meaning and labels remain readable on phones. The wording need not change to make the relationship more understandable. Propose directing the full inquiry flow to the consultation page while retaining a direct WhatsApp route.

### Konsultasi

Reuse the current consultation invitation and complete three-step explanation. Use the existing WhatsApp, email and office contact details. Include the existing disclaimer.

Confirmed: WhatsApp and email only. No inquiry form or delivery backend. Update the obsolete step-one reference to a contact form to “WhatsApp atau email”. Preserve the rest of the three-step explanation.

## Copy preservation rules

| Treatment | Working rule |
| --- | --- |
| Retain | Firm name, service substance, managing partner message, approved credentials, contact details and disclaimer stay grounded in current source copy |
| Reposition | Existing blocks may move to the page where they answer a visitor's question; record source and destination |
| Light edit | Propose punctuation, spelling, capitalization and navigation/button consistency as visible changes for review |
| New copy | Identify any new navigation labels, short bridges or instructions explicitly; keep them minimal |
| Substantive rewrite | Requires a separate content decision; do not replace the headline, alter service scope or invent promises |

Use a simple content map before implementation: source page/block → proposed page/section → retained/moved/light edit/new → exact change and reason → approval status. The reviewed source and browser observations remain the baseline.

Do not turn hidden template sections into public content. Language policy remains open: existing English and Indonesian wording should not be translated wholesale as an incidental part of the redesign.

## Connected visitor experience

The key journeys are Home → relevant practice → consultation; Home → partner/approach → consultation; and direct entry to a practice or strategic offering → consultation. Every page must make sense without a homepage introduction. A visitor can also reach WhatsApp or email directly.

Keep a recognizable shared header, logo, navigation, contact access and footer. Give pages distinct content compositions within that system. Repeated navigation must show the current page; browser Back should return visitors to their previous reading position where appropriate. Refreshing a nested URL must load that page; existing public links should remain usable. Prefer retaining the existing public URLs initially; any renamed URL needs an explicit redirect/deployment plan.

Opening content and contact actions should be usable without waiting for offscreen imagery or a decorative introduction. Motion must be interruptible by navigation and must not make the first visit a prerequisite for later pages. Direct entry, slow images, Back/Forward and repeat visits belong in the early connected slice.

For visual exploration, compare two possible treatments using the same preserved copy:

- An editorial, portrait-led treatment: visitors first understand the offer, then meet the partner and read his approach. A consistent image framing and navigation treatment connects the pages; the diagram becomes a secondary explanatory moment.
- An integrated-strategy treatment: the law, finance and management relationship receives more visual emphasis. On the strategic page, the three labeled perspectives can begin apart, come into relation and settle into one readable composition; scrolling or keyboard/touch navigation remains under visitor control.

Recommend exploring the portrait-led treatment first, with the relationship diagram developed within it. It uses the strongest available human asset while preserving an opportunity for expressive explanatory motion. This is a creative proposal, not approval of a style or animation. Both require representative page and phone compositions before choreography is specified.

The first inspectable exploration now includes a third, service-led alternative. ## Planning and delivery sequence

| Stage | Deliverable | Acceptance before advancing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Structure and content | Agreed page map, contact destination and source-to-section content map | Page responsibilities are clear; all public service copy has a destination; copy changes are visible |
| 2. Creative direction | Representative Home, substantive inner-page and consultation compositions, including phone/short laptop; motion storyboards | Client can judge real copy, logo, reading hierarchy and meaningful connections; no text-only claim of visual approval |

| 4. Remaining pages | About Us and Layanan Strategis integrated with shared navigation/contact flow | All seven practices and three strategic offerings remain accessible; portrait/diagram treatments work at agreed sizes |
| 5. Review and release preparation | Copy comparison, browser/device evidence and deployment plan | No unexplained copy drift; required routes, contact paths and agreed accessibility/performance checks pass |

Stages 3–5 are a proposed delivery order, not authorization to begin coding or publish. Detailed tasks, tests and allowed files follow once the structure, content, contact method and representative designs are approved. Existing jcodemunch licensing/source-exploration requirements apply before dependent engineering work.
